therapy-note-bench

LLM-written psychotherapy session notes, scored on two published protocols, against whatever e-INFRA CZ has deployed today. Source and method.

Judge calibration

Before any number here is trusted, the judge is scored against the two human annotators TN-Eval released, and the agreement appears here — including if it is bad. Not yet measured.

* Faithfulness is a 1–5 Likert rating. TN-Eval measured Krippendorff's alpha of 0.18 between trained therapists on it, so it is reported with that caveat and never used as a headline ranking.

† TRACE is a re-implementation with no human anchor: the authors' TRACE annotations are not published anywhere. It is reported beside the automatic metrics, never averaged into them.

Sessions counts notes a protocol could actually read. A model that writes a good note in the wrong shape loses sessions to its format, not to its clinical content — read the count before comparing the score.

Rows are only ever compared when they agree on harness version, prompt version, judge model and judge prompt version. A changed judge starts a new table rather than rewriting this one.
